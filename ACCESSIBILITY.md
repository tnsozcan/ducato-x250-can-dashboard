# Accessibility

I want the setup instructions and project information to be usable without relying only on the demo video or physical warning lamps.

## Current support

- Text-based English README and Turkish installation guide.
- Numbered connector pins and written CAN fields instead of wire color alone.
- Serial startup/error messages and counters in addition to physical gauge observations.
- Editable text source and launchers for Windows; English and Turkish issue reports are welcome.

## Known limitations

The physical cluster uses small markings, colored lamps, moving needles and audible warnings. I do not currently provide a screen-reader dashboard or text descriptions of every live gauge value. Firmware messages are largely ASCII Turkish. I have not completed a formal accessibility audit, assistive-technology test or WCAG conformance assessment. Video captions and audio descriptions have not been verified.

## Report a barrier

Open a [bug report](https://github.com/tnsozcan/ducato-x250-can-dashboard/issues/new?template=bug_report.md) and say which document or step is difficult to use, your browser/terminal or assistive technology, and the change that would help. You do not need to disclose a medical condition.
